## Make this now

**Ginger-shrimp fried rice with cabbage** (~15 min)

- Sauté minced ginger + onion in sesame oil, 1 min
- Add dried shrimp, toast 30 sec
- Toss in shredded cabbage, cook until just wilted (~3 min)
- Push to side; scramble two eggs in same pan
- Add cold leftover rice + soy sauce; fold everything, sear 2 min for crisp bottom
- Finish with a drizzle of sesame oil

Uses: rice, eggs, cabbage, soy, sesame, onion, ginger, dried shrimp.
