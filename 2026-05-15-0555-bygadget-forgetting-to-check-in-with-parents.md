# bygadget: I keep forgetting to check in with my parents in another city

_Context: Software engineer, comfortable with scripts, mobile-first daily, ~30 min/week budget._

---

## Restated problem

You said you forget to check in. The latent need is not better memory. It's a low-friction way to convert a fleeting *thought of* your parents into an *action toward* them. Most "forgetting" of relationships isn't amnesia — it's the friction between intention and action being slightly higher than the friction of inaction. Drop the action friction below the intention friction.

## Who has this

Most adults with parents in another city. The pattern is near-universal — sporadic guilt, intense visits, weeks of silence between. Not "just me."

## Smallest dissolve

**Form:** A small daily micro-action — push notification or morning text — that takes 30 seconds to act on.

- **Inputs (once at setup):** parent names, time-zone, what kinds of check-in count (text? photo? call? voice message?).
- **Outputs each morning:** a single line — *"Today's tiny: text Mom a photo of your breakfast."* Variety prevents staleness.
- **Where it runs:** a 30-line script you run weekly that generates the next 7 days; paste into phone reminders.

## Evolutionary frame

- **Capability transfer:** the gadget retrains the path from "thought of parents" → "action toward parents." After 30 days, the path is faster without the prompt. The user starts initiating without the nudge.
- **Failure mode:** the user dismisses the prompt and it becomes another ignored notification. Variety of actions is the safeguard — boredom kills calls; novelty keeps the muscle alive.
- **After 50 uses:** the user should be initiating ~half of parent-contact spontaneously, with the prompt only for the other half. The gadget gets *less* needed over time, not more.

## Starter artifact

```powershell
# parents-tiny.ps1 -- run weekly, generates 7 daily micro-actions
# Paste output into 7 morning reminders on your phone.

$actions = @(
    "text Mom a photo of your breakfast",
    "send Dad an old photo of the three of you",
    "call Mom for 5 minutes -- just to hear her voice",
    "ask Dad about something from his work he was proud of",
    "send Mom a song you think she'd like",
    "voice-message Dad about a problem you're solving",
    "tell Mom one specific thing you appreciate about her"
)

$shuffled = $actions | Get-Random -Count 7
$today = Get-Date
for ($i = 0; $i -lt 7; $i++) {
    $date = $today.AddDays($i).ToString('yyyy-MM-dd')
    Write-Output "$date : $($shuffled[$i])"
}
```

Run once a week. Paste output into phone reminders.

## What's next

- After two weeks: prune actions that feel forced; add ones from your week that worked.
- After a month: split into Mom-stream and Dad-stream if their preferred contact patterns differ.
- If you find yourself initiating without the prompt half the time, the gadget is working — *reduce* the frequency, don't increase it.
